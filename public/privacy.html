Family Calendar – Privacy

Family Calendar is a private wall calendar for one household. It is not a commercial service.

Google Calendar

When a family member taps “Connect Google Calendar”, the app asks Google for permission to read their calendars and to add, change or delete events they create from the wall. The app uses this only to show the family's events on the wall screen and to save events the family adds there.

Calendar events and the Google sign-in token are stored only in the family's own database. They are never sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising. Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Remove a calendar in Parents → Calendar connections, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing a calendar deletes its events from the app.
